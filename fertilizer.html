📊 Key Soil Nutrients Guide

🌱 Nitrogen (N)

Essential for vegetative vigor, stem elongation, leaf greenness, and early plant development.

🧪 Phosphorus (P)

Powers root expansion, seed germination, flowering vigor, and energy transfer within plant cells.

🌿 Potassium (K)

Regulates water uptake, builds drought resistance, and strengthens grain and fruit quality.

🧫 Soil pH

Optimal range 6.2 - 7.5. Acidic or alkaline extremes lock nutrients out of root absorption.
